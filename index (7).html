Diner House Rules

How to Play

  • Players take turns answering one multiple-choice question.
  • Correct: click a column (or press 1–7) to drop your disc. First to four in a row wins.
  • Incorrect: the other player goes next. The correct answer and a brief explanation appear after each guess.
  • With Jukebox enabled, every fifth question is 1950s pop culture. Answer correctly to choose Double Dip (two discs this turn) or a saved Do-Over Token.
  • A Do-Over Token lets you take a fresh history question after one wrong answer without losing your turn.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 answer questions; 1–7 choose a column once a disc is unlocked.
